01 / Project understanding

Knowing where crowds will build, before the day.

ExCeL London hosts many events at once, and needs to know in advance where crowding will build up so the right crowd management is in place on the day.

10Events a day, entered by ExCeL
ArrivalsVisitor counts, surveys and arrival patterns
RoutesWalkways, bridges, entrances and hall doors
RAGWhere crowding builds, and when
QueuesCustom House station queues after events, plus a year calendar and monthly summaries rated red, amber or green.

From one macro workbook to a secure cloud tool.

Following a study of crowd movement around the venue, Momentum Transport Consultancy built a pedestrian movement assignment tool in Excel, based on visitor counts, site surveys and its own experience, and has since updated it for 2026.

The spreadsheet has reached its limits. It must be opened in desktop Excel, is tied to a single calendar year, and cannot plan events outside 6am to midnight, use more than one hall door per event, add new entrances such as the Platinum Suite entrance, model entry checks at West Entrance, or test changes to the venue layout without being rebuilt.

ExCeL wants a secure cloud tool that gives the same answers as today through a live site map, and lets its team plan at any hour, use any entrance and test changes to the venue layout themselves.

08 / Relevant experience

A team that has built this kind of tool before.

The same team, technology and engine-first approach behind these tools would build ExCeL’s crowd planning tool.

Spatial Design Hub · In-house tool

Static Pedestrian Modelling Tool

Built to size pedestrian routes in stadiums, stations and masterplans. It turns drawn pedestrian networks and origin–destination demand into required route widths, Level of Service, and escalator and gateline requirements, across multiple scenarios.

Relevance: the ExCeL tool uses the same technology and the same engine-first approach, with the calculation engine proved against an existing spreadsheet before the screens are built.

Describes the tool’s capabilities; its results are calculated estimates from a static model, not micro-simulation.

Eastern City Cluster BID, working with Publica · Digital tool

Eastern City BID Construction Impacts Monitor

An interactive dashboard that tracks construction impacts across the district in one place. It shows every site’s start and end dates and which phases of work are underway, generates a report for each site, and calculates the bus routes, stops and workers affected in each impact zone.

Relevance: a map-led dashboard driven by dates and phases, with a report for each item, like the event calendar, time slider and day reports.

Describes the tool’s capabilities; affected routes, stops and worker numbers are calculated estimates.

With Momentum Transport Consultancy · Lead GIS and app developer

Micromobility Planner

An online tool that helps local authorities plan shared mobility schemes, evaluate operating areas and parking, and assess expansion options, combining public transport accessibility with Census 2021 data.

Relevance: Spatial Design Hub and Momentum turning a transport planning method into a web tool, as on this project.

Describes the tool’s published capabilities; no adoption, ridership or carbon outcomes are claimed.

Spatial Design Hub digital tool

Urban Access

10 / Assumptions and exclusions

What the fee is based on.

What we need from ExCeL and Momentum +
  • A named ExCeL lead to make decisions and sign off each stage.
  • Door, lane and layout information for the Platinum Suite and West Entrance, or access for a site visit.
  • A list of the ExCeL staff who need access, and who can edit or only view.
  • Time for user testing and two training sessions.
  • Handover from Momentum of the information behind the current spreadsheet.
  • Agreement between ExCeL, Momentum and Spatial Design Hub on who owns that information and how it can be used.
Assumptions +
  • The tool reproduces Momentum’s current method; it does not redo the original surveys or research.
  • Crowding levels, queue lengths and red, amber or green ratings are modelled estimates based on stated assumptions; they support crowd management planning, and decisions on the day remain with ExCeL’s teams.
  • The existing site map can be used as the base map, or ExCeL provides a plan to redraw it.
  • The tool is a defined first release. Feature priorities are agreed at inception to fit the fee, and further functionality is scoped separately.
  • The product is standalone and hosted in the UK. Any third-party licence or hosting need beyond the agreed scope is identified before commitment.
  • The fee includes one consolidated round of revisions following review, and remote meetings apart from the kick-off and training.
Exclusions +
  • Hosting, support and updates after handover, which are quoted separately.
  • Detailed crowd simulation (moving-person animation) and pedestrian microsimulation.
  • A live feed from ExCeL’s visitor counting system.
  • Future developments such as ExCeL Phase 3 and Dock Edge, until data is available.
  • Queue modelling at Prince Regent station and crowd density on the boulevard.
  • Microsoft single sign-on, mobile apps and real-time use on the event day.
  • New surveys or site counts, including the optional three-day check against visitor counts.
  • Travel and expenses, agreed in advance if needed.
