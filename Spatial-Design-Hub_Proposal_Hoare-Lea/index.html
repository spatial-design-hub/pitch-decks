01 / Project understanding

A better way to plan and evidence social value.

Hoare Lea’s model is grounded in public health evidence rather than counting activities and converting them into proxy values. The emphasis is on how an intervention is delivered, not only how many are delivered.

6Marmot public health principles
AimsSet out under each principle
InterventionsA library linked to each aim
EvidenceAcademic and grey literature log
4 + 3Amplifier effects, four process-based and three community-based, that increase an intervention’s impact and are reported qualitatively through case studies.

From scattered files to one platform.

Today the model lives in Excel workbooks, presentation slides and several Power BI reports, including the local needs analysis tool the team calls “the big one” and the Worthing SHINE amplifier case study.

Our two scoping workshops agreed the platform should be a proper web application with a database and mapping from the start, not a static website, so later phases can add reporting, geospatial upload and analysis without rebuilding what Phase 1 delivers.

08 / Relevant experience

Turning spatial evidence into usable tools.

The same team, stack and approach behind these tools would build the Social Value Model platform.

Eastern City Cluster BID, working with Publica · Digital tool

Eastern City BID Construction Impacts Monitor

An interactive dashboard tracking construction sites across the district, with a report for each site and the bus routes, stops and workers affected in each impact zone.

Relevance: the same pattern of map, dashboard and per-area reporting as the local needs mapping tool.

Describes the tool’s capabilities; affected routes, stops and worker numbers are calculated estimates.

With Momentum Transport Consultancy · Lead GIS and app developer

Micromobility Planner

An online tool for local authorities planning shared mobility schemes, combining public transport accessibility with Census 2021 data.

Relevance: Census data, area selection and summary indicators built for local authority users.

Describes the tool’s published capabilities; no adoption, ridership or carbon outcomes are claimed.

Spatial Design Hub digital tool

Urban Access

A tool bringing public transport accessibility, population context and amenity access together for planning and site assessment, with neighbourhood analysis and summary reports.

Relevance: the groundwork for Phase 3 catchments, daily needs access and facility testing.

Describes the tool’s capabilities; no time or cost savings are claimed.

Haven · Analysis and digital storyboard map

Seal Bay storyboard: accessibility and connectivity

An interactive storyboard map that walked Haven through the baseline accessibility and connectivity assessment of Seal Bay Resort, step by step, making a technical baseline easy for senior stakeholders to follow.

Relevance: the chapter-by-chapter, map-led storytelling behind the interactive case study template.

A baseline assessment and its presentation; visitor movement is modelled, and no delivered interventions are claimed.

Spatial Design Hub digital tool

Static Pedestrian Modelling Tool

A tool that turns drawn networks and origin–destination demand into required route widths, Level of Service, and escalator and gateline needs across multiple scenarios.

Relevance: a structured, step-by-step workspace where users enter inputs through forms and get consistent results, the same pattern as the case study workspace.

Describes the tool’s capabilities; its results are calculated estimates from a static model, not micro-simulation.

10 / Assumptions and exclusions

What the fee includes.

Assumptions +
  • Phase 1 is a defined first release using approved content. Feature priorities are agreed at inception to fit the fee, and further functionality is scoped separately.
  • Hoare Lea provides at inception the interventions and aims Excel, the evidence log, access to the Power BI reports, the draft UX document, brand guidelines and imagery, appropriately licensed.
  • Hoare Lea writes and approves all platform content, guides, templates, training material and case study content, including permission to publish the Worthing SHINE material.
  • The case study workspace allows several case study projects per account. If Hoare Lea prefers one project per login, this is agreed at inception within the same fee.
  • The local needs tool uses existing public data for England. New surveys or third-party data purchase require separate instruction.
  • Demographic outputs are aggregated in line with data licensing and disclosure requirements.
  • Paid access for commercial clients is invoiced by Hoare Lea outside the platform; Hoare Lea approves accounts in the admin area.
  • The fee includes one round of review on the designs and one consolidated round of revisions after user acceptance testing.
  • Hoare Lea names a product owner to give feedback and sign-offs within the programme.
  • The fee includes remote meetings. Travel and other expenses are excluded and would be agreed in advance if needed.
  • Any third-party licence or hosting need beyond the agreed scope is identified before commitment.
Exclusions +
  • Hosting costs and ongoing maintenance and support after handover, which are offered as described in the fee section.
  • Online payment or subscription billing.
  • Any scoring, credit or monetary valuation of social value, and quantification of the amplifier effects.
  • Writing platform content and developing training material.
  • The Phase 2 and Phase 3 items described in this proposal, including automated reporting, the AI research assistant and Scotland data.
  • Native mobile apps.
