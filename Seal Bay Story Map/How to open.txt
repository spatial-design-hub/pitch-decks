Seal Bay Story Map - Baseline Report v1.0
Spatial Design Hub and Momentum Transport Consultancy

HOW TO OPEN
1. Save this folder to your computer (if you received a zip, right-click it and choose "Extract All" first).
2. Double-click "Seal Bay Story Map.html". It opens in your web browser.
   Chrome, Edge or Firefox all work. Please don't open it in an email, Teams or SharePoint preview, as previews can't draw the maps.
3. Scroll down to move through the chapters. You can hover over map features for more detail, and click the photos and buttons.

NOTES
- The file works offline. With an internet connection it also shows a detailed street base map.
- If a red message appears saying the maps can't be drawn, the file has been opened in a preview. Save it and open it in your browser instead.
